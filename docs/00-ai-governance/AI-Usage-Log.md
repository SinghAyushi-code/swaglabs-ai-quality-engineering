## Outcome key

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Correct | Checked against evidence and found accurate |
| Corrected | Checked and found wrong in part; the human fixed or rejected it |
| Unverified | Not yet checked; must not be relied on |
| Process issue | The AI's way of working caused a problem, not its content |
