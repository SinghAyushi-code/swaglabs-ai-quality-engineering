### Framing Decision

SauceDemo is treated as the **Swag Labs online retail store**. We are the business that owns it. Consequences:

- Behaviour that a practice site would call "intended" (for example broken images for problem_user) is assessed as a **defect** against this vision.
- The special SauceDemo test users are **simulated production conditions**, not personas (see Section 4.3).
- We cannot change the SUT (CON-01), so defects are recorded and assessed, not fixed.

## 1. Executive Summary

Swag Labs has existing, registered customers but no online channel for buying its merchandise (BA-PRB). MVP Release 1 delivers a secure, login-only web store where a returning customer can browse a six-product catalogue, build a cart, check out and receive an on-screen order confirmation (BA-C-1 … BA-C-5, BA-J-1).

MVP 1 is deliberately narrow. It does not acquire new customers, support search, retain customers through accounts or promotions, or take real payment and full delivery details (BA-NS-1 … BA-NS-4). It is viable only if payment and delivery details are already held against each customer account (A-02, A-03). If those assumptions are contradicted, MVP 1 is a **controlled pilot**, not a general launch (BA-MVP-GAP).

The two highest business risks are publicly exposed login credentials (R-01) and orders that cannot be paid for or delivered (R-02, R-03). Success is judged on purchase-journey reliability, order-total accuracy and access control, measured through testing. Commercial KPIs are defined but cannot be measured until real traffic exists (Section 11).

## 2. Product Vision

**Vision statement**

> For existing Swag Labs customers who want to buy branded merchandise quickly, the Swag Labs Store is a secure online shop that takes a customer from login to confirmed order in a few steps. Unlike ordering through offline channels, it is available on demand and shows the customer exactly what they are paying before they commit.

### 7.4 Scope Challenge

MVP 1 contains items not needed to sell (MVP-13 … MVP-15). It also lacks items a general launch needs (OOS-04). The release-readiness position is therefore set in advance:

- **Go** for MVP 1 as a controlled release to existing customers, if A-02 and A-03 are Confirmed or accepted as residual risk.
- **No-Go** for a general public launch until OOS-04 is delivered and R-01 is resolved.

## 11. Success Criteria

Release criteria can be measured within the portfolio. Commercial KPIs cannot, because there is no real traffic (CON-04). They are defined so the measures exist from day one, but they cannot pass or fail MVP 1.

### 11.1 Release Success Criteria (measurable now)

| ID | Criterion | Target | Evidence | Objective | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SC-01 | The purchase journey from login to confirmation succeeds for standard_user | 100% of runs pass | Automated test results | BV-OBJ-01 | BA-J-1 |
| SC-02 | Overview total = subtotal + tax, to 2 decimal places, for every cart combination tested | 100% correct | Automated and manual tests | BV-OBJ-03 | BA-C-5, BA-V-1 |
| SC-03 | Unauthenticated access to every protected page redirects to login; locked_out_user is refused | 100% | Automated tests | BV-OBJ-02 | BA-J-2 |
| SC-04 | Leaving each of first name, last name or postal code empty shows an error and blocks checkout | 3 of 3 fields | Automated tests | BV-OBJ-04 | BA-J-3 |
| SC-05 | The cart badge equals the number of items after every add or remove, from every location | 100% | Automated tests | BV-OBJ-05 | BA-J-4 |
| SC-06 | Each degraded condition (problem, performance_glitch, error, visual) is tested, and its defects are logged and assessed | 4 of 4 conditions | Defect log, test report | BV-OBJ-06 | BA-TU-3 … 6 |
| SC-07 | Open Critical or High defects on Must scope items at release | 0, or each one accepted by the PO with written justification | Release readiness report | BV-OBJ-01 | BA-MVP-M |
| SC-08 | High-impact assumptions (A-02, A-03, A-05) Confirmed or accepted as residual risk before Go | 3 of 3 | Assumption log | All | BA-MVP-GAP |

### 11.2 Commercial KPIs (post-launch only – not measurable in MVP 1)

| ID | KPI | Definition | Target | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KPI-01 | Purchase conversion | Sessions reaching confirmation ÷ logged-in sessions | To be set by the PO after baseline data exists | BV-OBJ-01 |
| KPI-02 | Checkout abandonment | Sessions starting checkout without reaching confirmation ÷ sessions starting checkout | To be set by the PO | BV-OBJ-04 |
